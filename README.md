# Sales Performance Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes historical sales data to identify revenue trends, profitability drivers, top-performing products, customer segments, and geographic markets.

The project demonstrates an end-to-end data analytics workflow using **SQL and Power BI**.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

The business needed to understand its sales and profitability performance across products, customers, markets, and time.

The analysis aimed to answer:

* How is revenue and profit performing?
* Which products and categories drive sales?
* Which markets generate the most revenue?
* Which customer segments are most valuable?
* How has performance changed over time?
* Which products have high revenue but low profitability?

## 🔍 Key Findings

### 1. Revenue Performance

The analysis identified strong overall revenue and profit performance across the period covered by the dataset.

### 2. Product Performance

Bikes were the primary revenue driver, while Accessories generated a significantly higher profit margin.

### 3. Geographic Performance

The United States was the strongest revenue-generating market, with California among the strongest-performing regions.

### 4. Customer Segments

Customers aged 35–64 represented the highest-value customer segment based on revenue and profit contribution.

### 5. Yearly Performance

2015 was the strongest full year in terms of revenue and profit.

### 6. Profitability

The analysis demonstrated that high revenue does not necessarily mean high profitability. Product-level margin analysis was therefore used to identify potential optimization opportunities.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

1. Increase cross-selling of high-margin accessories.
2. Prioritize inventory for high-performing bike products.
3. Develop targeted campaigns for high-value customer segments.
4. Focus expansion efforts on high-performing geographic markets.
5. Review high-revenue products with relatively low profit margins.
6. Investigate duplicate records before using the data for financial reporting.
7. Use the Power BI dashboard for continuous performance monitoring.
